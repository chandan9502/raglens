## Notes on the data

- Only the QA task of RAGTruth is used (not summarisation or data-to-text). A response is *unsupported* if annotators marked at least one hallucinated span in it. That includes spans annotators tagged `implicit_true` (true, but not stated in the passages), because RAGLens checks support by the passages.
- RAGTruth's prompt told models to reply "Unable to answer based on given passages." when the passages lacked the answer. RAGLens recognises refusals with its built-in, general patterns; no dataset-specific phrase is passed.
- HaluEval QA labels come from how the data was built: the HotpotQA answer is *supported*, a ChatGPT-written plausible wrong answer is *unsupported*. Right answers are short (median 2 words) while wrong ones are sentences (median 9 words), a difference that suits a word-overlap check. A few of the "wrong" answers are refusals ("The television series is not specified."); RAGLens marks those `abstained`, which counts as a miss here.
- Thresholds were chosen with `raglens calibrate` (best F1 on a 0.01 grid) on RAGTruth train. The test split and HaluEval were never used for tuning.
- The model-based detectors evaluated in the [RAGTruth paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.00396) report much higher response-level F1 (across all three tasks, so not directly comparable). RAGLens is a no-model baseline; these numbers show what word overlap alone can do.
